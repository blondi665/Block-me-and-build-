# Block Me & Build

Tower-defense wall builder. Place blocks to funnel a swarm away from the core.

A self-contained HTML5 canvas game. No build step.

## How to play

**Touch / click:** tap an empty cell to spend energy on a wall. Tap a wall to upgrade or sell. Energy regenerates.

Enemies path around your walls. Keep them off the core. Survive rising waves.

**Keys:** `M` mute · `P` or `Esc` pause · Space or Enter starts

Qualifying scores register on the **Seeker fleet** when this game is opened from Ionwake.

## Seeker / mobile

Portrait 9:16, 44px tap targets, safe-area padding. Built for Seeker mobile shells.

## Open

Open `index.html` in a browser, or play it from the Ionwake fleet arcade.
